# Medplum Earns Drummond Validated FHIRplace Silver Status for CRD

Medplum v5 has earned **Drummond Validated™ – FHIRplace Silver (CRD)** status for both **provider and payer roles** in the **Q3 2026** testing cycle. The validation recognizes confirmed interoperability with multiple partners for Coverage Requirements Discovery (CRD) in a structured, production-representative FHIR testing environment.

{/* truncate */}

## From Participation to Validation

In July, we [announced our participation in FHIRplace](/blog/fhirplace-participant-2026). We have now achieved Silver CRD validation in two distinct tested roles. Drummond determined both statuses on **October 1, 2026**:

| Product and version | Role     | Validation ID  |
| ------------------- | -------- | -------------- |
| Medplum v5          | Provider | FP-2026-Q3-001 |
| Medplum v5          | Payer    | FP-2026-Q3-002 |

FHIRplace evaluates exchanges between participating systems through a testing matrix. For Medplum, the validated workflow is CRD: discovering a payer's coverage requirements during a provider's ordering workflow. Testing in both roles exercises Medplum on each side of that exchange.

The validation is specific to Medplum v5, these two roles, CRD, the Silver tier, and Q3 2026. Other versions, use cases, and later quarters are outside its scope. Validation records and testing reports are available on [Drummond's FHIRplace Validated Products and Participants page](https://www.drummondgroup.com/certified-products/fhirplace-validated-products/). This validation does not represent an endorsement by any governing body.

## Supporting Prior Authorization Implementations

This milestone provides interoperability evidence for teams building CRD workflows with Medplum. Our [CMS-0057-F compliance page](/docs/compliance/cms-0057-f) includes the validation seal and details alongside the payer requirements. Our separate [HTI-4 page](/docs/compliance/hti-4) explains the ONC certification criteria.

Medplum is pursuing ONC certification for all three ePA criteria: CRD, Documentation Templates and Rules (DTR), and Prior Authorization Support (PAS). We have not announced a certification timeline. The FHIRplace CRD validation is separate from that certification work and does not establish DTR or PAS validation or comprehensive CMS-0057-F compliance.

To begin testing with Medplum, see our [electronic prior authorization guide](/docs/integration/electronic-prior-auth), or contact [hello@medplum.com](mailto:hello@medplum.com).
