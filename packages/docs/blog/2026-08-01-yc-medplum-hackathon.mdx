## Judging Criteria

Hacks will be judged on two criteria:

1. **Potential impact:** does the hack meaningfully improve patient care, clinician experience, or the quality of care delivered? We are looking for hacks in the spirit of Agentic Healthcare: intelligent, standards compliant, automated, even voice enabled, enhancing clinicians rather than adding to their workload.
2. **Effective use of the provided technologies:** how well does the hack use Deepgram, Medplum, moss.dev, and/or Stedi?
