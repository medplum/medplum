## Morning Session

**9:00 AM - Breakfast**

### 9:30 AM - Keynote: Medplum Past, Present and Future

<img src="https://github.com/codyebberson.png" alt="Cody Ebberson" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Cody Ebberson](https://github.com/codyebberson)**

A state of the union on the Medplum platform: what launched in the past year, and what is launching today.

_Themes: 🧩 Platform, 🗺️ Roadmap_

### 10:15 AM - Stanford Biodesign: Agentic Coding Healthcare IT

<img src="/img/avatars/aydin-zahedivash.webp" alt="Aydin Zahedivash" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="/img/avatars/vishnu-ravi.webp" alt="Vishnu Ravi" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Vishnu Ravi](https://med.stanford.edu/profiles/vishnu-ravi)**, **[Aydin Zahedivash](https://med.stanford.edu/profiles/aydin-zahedivash)**

Agentic coding and clinician input are driving a new wave of innovation, as seen in [CS 342: Building for Digital Health](https://cs342.stanford.edu/) at Stanford this year.

_Themes: 🤖 AI, 💻 Agentic Coding, 🩺 Clinician-Led Innovation_

**10:30 AM - Coffee Break**

### 10:45 AM - Scaling to 100M Patients

<img src="https://github.com/mattlong.png" alt="Matt Long" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="https://github.com/maddyli.png" alt="Maddy Li" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Matt Long](https://github.com/mattlong)**, **[Maddy Li](https://github.com/maddyli)**

New platform features enable workflows at a national scale. Scale happens in multiple dimensions, some literal (patients, API calls) and some more related to access control, observability, and governance. This session covers how sharding, the data warehouse, asynchronous batches, access controls, websockets, and expanded use of standards drive scale.

_Themes: 📈 Scale, 🏗️ Infrastructure, 📐 Standards, 🔐 Access Control_

### 11:30 AM - Product Showcase

<img src="https://github.com/everett-williams.png" alt="Everett Williams" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="https://github.com/noahsilas.png" alt="Noah Silas" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Everett Williams](https://github.com/everett-williams)**, **[Noah Silas](https://github.com/noahsilas)**

Platform enhancements have enabled new workflows, and we will showcase them: scheduling, DICOM, health information exchange, medications, agents and voice, financial and revenue cycle, prior authorization, and marketplace.

_Themes: 📦 Product, 🔄 Workflows_

**12:00 PM - Lunch Break**

## Afternoon Session

### 1:00 PM - Scheduling Agents for Healthcare Operations

<img src="https://github.com/codyhall.png" alt="Cody Hall" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Cody Hall](https://github.com/codyhall)**, Unity AI

The team at Unity AI will discuss their experience building and scaling a healthcare service that manages complex scheduling workflows over the phone. They will cover the architecture of the application, caregiver access and logic, and how the voice system is integrated.

_Themes: 🤖 AI, 🦾 Agents, 📞 Voice Scheduling, 🧰 Developer Platform, ⚡ Realtime_

### 1:25 PM - Voice Applications: Medplum + Deepgram

<img src="https://github.com/dg-edcharbeneau.png" alt="Ed Charbeneau" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="https://github.com/techdavidy.png" alt="David Yanez" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Ed Charbeneau](https://github.com/dg-edcharbeneau)**, [Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/), moderated by **[David Yanez](https://github.com/techdavidy)**

Ed from Deepgram will showcase what he built in a one day hack: what Deepgram is, where it touches Medplum, and a demo of the application. Watch the [demo video](https://youtu.be/zbc5M5-2zms) and browse the source at [deepgram-devs/medplum-patient-intake-deepgram](https://github.com/deepgram-devs/medplum-patient-intake-deepgram).

_Themes: 🤖 AI, 🦾 Agents, 🎙️ Voice, 🛠️ Developer Experience, ✅ Consents_

### 1:50 PM - Open Source for Healthcare

<img src="/img/avatars/stuart.webp" alt="Stuart Parmenter" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Stuart Parmenter](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stuartparmenter)**

Stuart Parmenter, former CTO of One Medical and an early team member at Mozilla, has a unique perspective on the role of open source in healthcare and on delivering care at scale. Advancements in LLMs and AI are ushering in a new era for healthcare.

_Themes: 🤖 AI, 🦾 Agents, 🌱 Open Source_

**2:15 PM - Stretch Break**

### 2:30 PM - Fireside Chat: Realtime Applications in Healthcare (PACE)

<img src="/img/avatars/yang-su.webp" alt="Yang Su" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="/img/avatars/meena-ramachandran.webp" alt="Meena Ramachandran" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Yang Su](https://www.linkedin.com/in/suyang1/)**, **[Meena Ramachandran](https://www.linkedin.com/in/meenaramachandran/)**, [Seen Health](https://www.seenhealth.org/)

### 2:50 PM - 9 products on Medplum: modular foundations I wish I'd had on day one

<img src="https://github.com/fjsj.png" alt="Flávio Juvenal" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="https://github.com/techdavidy.png" alt="David Yanez" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> <img src="https://github.com/kevinwadeshaw.png" alt="Kevin Shaw" width="50" height="50" style={{borderRadius: '50%'}} /> **[Flávio Juvenal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/flaviojuvenal)**, Vinta Software, **[David Yanez](https://github.com/techdavidy)**, **[Kevin Shaw](https://github.com/kevinwadeshaw)**

Flávio has led nine Medplum implementations, and he will share the learnings from all of them: what he would build once and reuse, and what he wishes had been in place on day one. He is joined by David Yanez and Kevin Shaw, who lead the [Medplum Provider](/docs/provider) experience.

_Themes: 🧰 Developer Platform, 🚀 Implementation_
