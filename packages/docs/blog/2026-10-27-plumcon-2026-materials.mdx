# PlumCon 2026 Materials

On September 3, 2026 Medplum hosted PlumCon 2026 in San Francisco. Thank you to everyone who attended, spoke, and shared demos!

Below you'll find slides and available recordings for each session from the [agenda](/blog/plumcon-2026), followed by demos from the community. Continue the conversation in the [PlumCon Discord channel](https://discord.gg/2QXPprmsV).

### Community Demos

Recorded demos shared by the community. Thank you to every team who contributed a video.

#### [Everself](https://everself.com/)

Orbit, a custom EHR that puts every prescription, note, message, lab, and smart scale reading on one chronological patient timeline for longitudinal obesity care. Read the [case study](/blog/everself-case-study).
