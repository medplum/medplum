# Submitting Claims to Candid Health

Medplum Provider can submit professional claims directly to [Candid Health](https://www.candidhealth.com) from a Visit's "Details & Billing" tab. Candid forwards the claim to the payer and reports its status back into Medplum. This video walks through the complete workflow, from billing setup to a submitted claim:

:::info[Prerequisites]
The Candid Health integration is an enterprise feature. Please [contact the Medplum team](mailto:hello@medplum.com) to have it enabled in your project. Once enabled, the `billing` feature on your [Project](/docs/access/projects#settings) turns on the **Billing Settings** page in Medplum Provider.

You should also be familiar with [Visits](./visits) and [Care Templates](./visits#setting-up-care-templates-via-medplum-app), since Charge Items on a Visit are what populate the claim.
:::

## Overview

Submitting a claim from Medplum Provider involves three stages:

1. **Billing setup** (one time): register your billing organizations and rendering practitioners with Candid, and import the payers you bill.
2. **Patient setup**: record the patient's insurance Coverage so it can be selected on the claim.
3. **Claim submission** (per Visit): document the Visit, sign and lock it, review the claim, and submit it to Candid.

Under the hood, the app calls the `$candid-submit-claim` operation described in the [Candid Health integration docs](/docs/integration/candid/claim-submission). That page documents the FHIR resources and identifiers Candid requires, which is useful if you are importing data or building your own front end.

## Billing Setup

Open **Settings → Billing Settings** (`/Settings/Billing`) in Medplum Provider. The page has four tabs:

- **Billing Organizations**: the entities that bill payers. Each needs an NPI, a Tax ID (EIN), and a complete address. Saving an organization here also registers it with Candid as an organization provider. Payer contracts are still set up in the Candid portal; open an organization to see which contracts are active.
- **Billing Practitioners**: the practitioners who render services. Each needs an NPI and a NUCC taxonomy code. Saving a practitioner registers them with Candid and links them as a rendering provider on the billing organization's contracts.
- **Payer Directory**: search Candid's payer directory and click **Import selected** to save payers as Organizations in your project.
- **Enrolled Payers**: the payers you have imported, with their identifiers and clearinghouse support. See [Payer Directory](/docs/integration/candid/payer-directory) for details.

## Patient Coverage

Before a claim can be submitted, the patient needs an insurance [Coverage](/docs/api/fhir/resources/coverage) that points to one of your enrolled payers and carries the member (subscriber) ID. Add coverage from the patient's profile in Medplum Provider, or import it with the rest of your [patient data](./getting-started#importing-data).

You can also run an eligibility check against the coverage from the patient's Coverage page. See [Eligibility Check](/docs/integration/candid/eligibility-check) for what the check returns.

## Submitting a Claim

1. **Document the Visit.** Complete the Visit's tasks and questionnaires, then open the "Details & Billing" tab. Confirm the Charge Items generated by your Care Template, add at least one diagnosis, and select the billing organization.
2. **Sign and lock the Visit.** The **Submit Claim** and **Export Claim** buttons are disabled until the Visit's chart note has been signed and locked.
3. **Review the claim.** Click **Submit Claim**. The review dialog shows the patient, the rendering practitioner, the diagnoses, and the total. Choose **Insurance pay** and select the Coverage(s) to bill, or choose **Self-pay** if the patient is paying out of pocket.
4. **Submit to Candid.** Click **Submit to Candid**. On success, the app saves a [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) with Candid's encounter and claim identifiers and shows a "Claim Submitted" notification.

After submission, the "Details & Billing" tab replaces the submit button with a claim status panel. It shows the Candid claim status, the submitted amount and time, and a **View Claim on Candid** button that opens the claim in the Candid portal. The status refreshes from Candid when you reopen the Visit, so you can track the claim from `waiting_for_provider` through to `finalized_paid` without leaving Medplum Provider.

:::tip
If you do not use Candid, the same tab lets you **Export Claim** as a CMS 1500 PDF, or submit to [Stedi](/docs/integration/stedi/claim-submission/professional-claims) when that integration is enabled.
:::
