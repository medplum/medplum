## Usage

The [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource is a flat, possibly ordered collection of records. [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resources are used in many places, including allergies, medications, alerts, family history, medical history, etc. [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resources can be used to support patient-specific clinical lists as well as lists that manage workflows such as tracking patients, managing teaching cases, etc. Resources supported by the [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource can be homogeneous – consisting of only one type of resource (e.g. allergy lists) as well as heterogeneous – containing a variety of resources (e.g. a problem list including [Conditions](/docs/api/fhir/resources/condition), [AllergyIntolerances](/docs/api/fhir/resources/allergyintolerance), recent [Procedures](/docs/api/fhir/resources/procedure), etc.).

[Lists](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) will typically include references to the resources that make up the list, however in some cases the details of the content of the list might be expressed in narrative only; e.g. a text record of a family history. The [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource is only needed if there is a need to filter the set of resources by a mechanism that cannot be accomplished via a simple query; e.g. there is no need to have a list for all [AllergyIntolerances](/docs/api/fhir/resources/allergyintolerance) that exist on a server for a given patient. However, [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) is an appropriate mechanism to provide a filtered list of the subset of [AllergyIntolerances](/docs/api/fhir/resources/allergyintolerance) that are deemed to be "current". [Lists](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) are allowed to contain other [Lists](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list), to create a nested collection of [Lists](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list).

Querying a [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) of resources such as [AllergyIntolerance](/docs/api/fhir/resources/allergyintolerance), [Condition](/docs/api/fhir/resources/condition) or [Medication](/docs/api/fhir/resources/medication)\-related resources is different than querying the resource-specific endpoint. For example, a [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) of [AllergyIntolerance](/docs/api/fhir/resources/allergyintolerance) resources would represent a curated point-in-time snapshot of the patient's allergies and intolerances. On the other hand, querying the [AllergyIntolerance](/docs/api/fhir/resources/allergyintolerance) endpoint would typically produce a larger set of records as it would both be non-curated (potentially containing duplicate or out-of-date records) and current - generated based on information as of "now" rather than the last time a human manually revised the [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource instance. Which mechanism is most appropriate for data retrieval will vary by use-case. In some cases, systems might not have an appropriate curated [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) to query.

Note that the presence of an item in a [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource SHALL NOT change the meaning of any information that would be understood by looking at the item outside the context of the [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list), because items may be accessed directly outside the [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) by RESTful means or after a document is processed. For example, a [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) with a code that means "refuted conditions" cannot have items that are [Condition](/docs/api/fhir/resources/condition) resources that do not have a `verificationStatus` of [refuted](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/valueset-condition-clinical.html#refuted).

## Relationships

There are five mechanisms in FHIR for communicating collections of resources:

- This [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource - enumerates a flat collection of resources and provides features for managing the collection. While a particular [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) instance may represent a "snapshot", from a business process perspective the notion of "List" is dynamic – items are added and removed over time. The [List](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) resource references other resources. [Lists](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) may be curated and have specific business meaning.
- The [Group](/docs/api/fhir/resources/group) resource - defines a group of specific people, animals, devices, etc. by enumerating them, or by describing qualities that group members have. The group resource refers to other resources, possibly implicitly. [Groups](/docs/api/fhir/resources/group) are intended to be acted upon or observed as a whole; e.g. performing therapy on a group, calculating risk for a group, etc. This resource will commonly be used for public health (e.g. describing an at-risk population), clinical trials (e.g. defining a test subject pool) and similar purposes.
- The [Composition](/docs/api/fhir/resources/composition) resource - defines a set of healthcare-related information that is assembled together into a single logical document that provides a single coherent statement of meaning, establishes its own context and that has clinical attestation with regard to who is making the statement. The [Composition](/docs/api/fhir/resources/composition) resource provides the basic structure of a FHIR [document](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/documents.html). The full content of the document is expressed using a bundle. [Compositions](/docs/api/fhir/resources/composition) will often reference [Lists](/docs/api/fhir/resources/list) as the focus of particular sections.
- The [Bundle](/docs/api/fhir/resources/bundle) resource - is an infrastructure container for a group of resources. It does not have a narrative and is used to group collections of resources for transmission, persistence or processing (e.g. messages, documents, transactions, query responses, etc.) The content of bundles is typically algorithmically determined for a particular exchange or persistence purpose.
- The [DomainResource](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/domainresource.html).`contained` element - allows multiple resources to be nested inside any DomainResource. This is a special type of grouping where the grouped resources lose independent existence - they no longer have their own identifiers, can't easily be queried independently, etc. Use of this grouping is a technical mechanism for managing the independence of resources and has no impact on meaning. Contained, bundled, and remotely referenced resources convey the same meaning.
