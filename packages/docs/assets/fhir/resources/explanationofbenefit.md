## Usage

The [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) (EOB) resource combines key information from a [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim), a [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) and optional [Account](/docs/api/fhir/resources/account) information to inform a patient of the goods and services rendered by a provider and the settlement made under the patient's coverage in respect of that [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim). The [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) resource may also be used as a resource for data exchange for bulk data analysis, as the resource encompasses [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim), [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) and [Coverage](/docs/api/fhir/resources/coverage)/Eligibility information.

This is the logical combination of the [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim), [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) and some [Coverage](/docs/api/fhir/resources/coverage) accounting information in respect of a single payor prepared for consumption by the subscriber and/or patient. It is not simply a series of pointers to referred-to content models, is a physical subset scoped to the adjudication by a single payor which details the services rendered, the amounts to be settled and to whom, and optionally the coverage allowed under the policy and the amounts used to date.

Typically the EOB is only used to convey [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim) (use=claim) and the associated [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) information to patients or subscribers. It may also be used to convey consolidated predetermination and preauthorization request and response information to patients or subscribers. An EOB will never be created for patient or subscriber information exchange if an error was detected in the [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim).

It is also recognized that "EOB" is a term that carries additional meaning in certain areas of the industry. When the resource was originally being developed there was substantial discussion about the adoption of an alternative name for the resource but after much discussion it was resolved that the [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) name has the advantage of familiarity that has been proven through the early adoption of the resource for multiple purposes.

**Note:** when creating profiles for EOB as a patient focused information exchange the payment details, other than date, should be excluded if the payee is the provider as that would leak business confidential information.

**Note:** the EOB **SHALL NOT** be used as a replacement for a [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) when responding to [Claims](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim). Only the [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) contains the appropriate adjudication information for a payor response to a [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim).

The [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) resource is an "event" resource from a FHIR workflow perspective - see [Workflow Event.](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/workflow.html#event)

## Relationships

The [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) resource is for reporting out to patients or transferring data to patient centered applications, such as patient health Record (PHR) application, the [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) should be used instead of the [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim) and [ClaimResponse](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claimresponse) resources as those resources may contain provider and payer specific information which is not appropriate for sharing with the patient.

When using the resources for reporting and transferring claims data, which may have originated in some standard other than FHIR, the [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim) resource is useful if only the request side of the information exchange is of interest. If, however, both the request and the adjudication information is to be reported then the [ExplanationOfBenefit](/docs/api/fhir/resources/explanationofbenefit) should be used instead.

The [Claim](/docs/api/fhir/resources/claim) resource is used to request the adjudication and/or authorization of a set of healthcare-related goods and services for a patient against the patient's insurance coverages, or to request what the adjudication would be for a supplied set of goods or services should they be actually supplied to the patient.

When requesting whether the patient's coverage is inforce, whether it is valid at this or a specified date, or requesting the benefit details or preauthorization requirements associated with a coverage [CoverageEligibilityRequest](/docs/api/fhir/resources/coverageeligibilityrequest) should be used instead.
