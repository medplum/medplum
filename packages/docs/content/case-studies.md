{{< feature title="AI" icon="outline/code" >}}
Medplum is a best of breed EHR for integrating AI. Our customers build sophisticated, high-fidelity [AI driven applications](/blog/tags/ai) on an open source platform with much attention to detail.
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature title="Specialty EHR" icon="outline/heart-rate-monitor" >}}
Medplum powers many specialty electronic health record systems and other purpose-built healthcare apps. Implmentations include solutions across specialties: [pediatrics](/blog/tags/pediatrics),[radiology](/blog/tags/radiology), [geriatrics](/blog/ensage-case-study), cardiac care and more.
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature title="Diagnostics" icon="outline/flask" >}}
Diagnostics providers need highly programmable system that can manage data securely at scale. Medplum provides solutions for [laboratory](/blog/ro-case-study), medical device, imaging, [remote patient monitoring](/blog/codex-and-the-power-of-g10) and more.
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature title="Interop" icon="outline/exchange" >}}
Reliable and transparent [integrations](/docs/integration) are built on Medplum. Integrate many systems on the same unified platform. Medplum's [bot framework](/docs/bots) and [industry standard authentication](/docs/auth) offering speed up development and ensure that integrations really work.
{{< /feature >}}
